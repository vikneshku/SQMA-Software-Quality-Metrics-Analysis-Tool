Formula

MI = max(0, min(100, (171 - 5.2·ln(HV) - 0.23·CC - 16.2·ln(avgFnLOC)) × 100 / 171)), with HV = avgFnLOC × log2(12)

Calculated per file from its average function size (avgFnLOC) and average complexity (CC); the project value is the LOC-weighted average of the file values. Check: avg function 20 lines & CC 4 = 58; 50 lines & CC 25 = 44; 100 lines & CC 30 = 34. HV is a documented approximation, not a full Halstead tokenizer.
